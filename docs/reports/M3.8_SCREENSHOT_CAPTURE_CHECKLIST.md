## 2. Capture Protocol
- **Resolution:** Full HD (1920x1080) or retina viewport.
- **Browser:** Chrome / Safari in Dark Mode.
- **Save Location:** `data/screenshots/` (e.g. `01_login.png`, `02_dashboard.png`, `08_evidence_verified.png`).
